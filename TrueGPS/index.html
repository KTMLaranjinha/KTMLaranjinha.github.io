Quick manual

  1. Swipe to switch HUDs.
  2. Open Settings using one of these methods:
    1. Long-press the clock in the upper-left corner.
    2. Long-press the screen and select Open Settings.
    3. Tap the Settings button beside the clock.
  3. Explore the options in each Settings tab. There are many!
  4. A Premium subscription unlocks features as they are released.
  5. In the Free version, spend credits to unlock paid features. Earn 5 credits by watching complete videos.
  6. Long-press the screen for HUD options such as duplicate, delete, and export.
